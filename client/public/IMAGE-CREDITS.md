# Image credits — ShopSmart product photographs

All product images are genuine photographs downloaded from **Wikimedia Commons** and
stored locally in `client/public/products/`. They are used under the Creative Commons
licence listed for each file (attribution required) or are in the public domain.
No AI-generated or stock-illustration images are used. Nothing is hotlinked at runtime,
so the site works with no internet connection.

The photographs show real hardware; the ShopSmart product names, brands, prices and
descriptions are fictional.
